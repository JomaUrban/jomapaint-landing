What we cut.

Sheet in five materials up to 6 mm, from one-off parts to series. If you are going to bend, weld or paint it afterwards, we give you the flat part ready for it.

  • Sheet metal laser cutting

    Bodor fibre laser with a large-format bed. Outlines, holes, slots and cut-outs in a single pass, with a clean edge.

  • Cold-rolled steel

    DC01, DC03 and DC04 (EN 10130): general-purpose mild steel sheet, smooth surface and consistent thickness.

  • Electrogalvanised steel

    DC01+ZE (EN 10152): thin, even zinc layer, a good base for painting.

  • Galvanised steel

    DX51D+Z (EN 10346): hot-dip zinc for parts that live outdoors.

  • Stainless steel

    1.4301 (AISI 304) and 1.4404 (AISI 316L) for food, chemical and outdoor use.

  • Aluminium

    EN AW-5754 and 1050A: light, corrosion-resistant and easy to bend.

    What you can ask for, in numbers.

    The accuracy of a laser-cut part is specified with standards, not adjectives. We tell you what tolerance is reasonable and what changes with thickness.

    Tolerances · ISO 9013 and ISO 2768

    ISO 9013 classifies the edge quality of a thermal cut: perpendicularity and roughness. Dimensions without a tolerance on the drawing follow ISO 2768-1 (class m, medium, the usual one for sheet metal). If a dimension is critical, mark it on the drawing and we review it before giving a price.

    1. ISO 9013 · edge quality
    2. ISO 2768-1 · untoleranced dimensions
    3. Your drawing · critical dimensions

    Before you send us your file

    • DXF or DWG at 1:1 scale and in millimetres: one part per file, in model space.
    • Closed outlines with no duplicate lines: the laser follows the geometry exactly as drawn.
    • Text converted to outlines if it has to be cut; if it is notes, we ignore it.
    • Holes at least as large as the sheet thickness (1.5 times in stainless and aluminium).
    • If the part is going to be bent, send us the flat pattern or the sheet metal STEP.
    • State material, thickness and quantity, and mark critical dimensions on the drawing.

    Frequently asked questions

    What machines do you cut with?+

    Bodor P-series fibre lasers with a large-format bed. They cut sheet up to 6 mm thick; the maximum depends on the material.

    What materials do you cut?+

    Cold-rolled steel (DC01–DC04), electrogalvanised steel (DC01+ZE), galvanised steel (DX51D+Z), stainless steel (304 and 316L) and aluminium (5754 and 1050A). Each service page lists grades and standards.

    What file do I need to upload?+

    For flat parts, a DXF or DWG at 1:1 scale in millimetres. We also accept STEP and PDF drawings with all dimensions. The details are in the file preparation guide.

    How long does a price take?+

    Under 24 working hours from when we receive the request. The clock runs Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 15:00 (Spanish time).

    Do you bend or paint the parts?+

    Bending, no: we cut the flat part. If you also want it powder coated, tick it in the request and we coordinate it with a paint line in the same plant.

    Do you ship outside Spain?+

    Yes. We ship anywhere in Spain and across Europe, with carriage included in the quote. You can also collect the parts in Legutio.

    Is there a minimum order?+

    Tell us with your file: the price depends on material, thickness, cut length and quantity, and you see it in the quote before you decide.

    Are my files confidential?+

    Yes. We only use them to prepare your quote and make your order, and we do not share them with third parties.
