How we handle a prototype

  1. 01You upload the fileThe flat part as DXF, or as STEP if you work in 3D, one per file.
  2. 02We review manufacturabilityHoles, distances, bridges and thickness. If something can't be cut or could be made cheaper, we tell you.
  3. 03We send you the priceIn writing, within 24 working hours.
  4. 04We cutThe part leaves flat, ready for you to bend, weld or assemble.

Iterate without losing time

At the prototype stage, every change counts. As there is no part-specific tooling, a new version only costs a new file. State the version and what has changed in the notes so the review is faster.
