Laser, plasma, waterjet or punching

Each process has its territory. Laser wins in thin and medium sheet with complex geometry; plasma in thick plate; waterjet when heat is a problem; punching in long runs.

When to choose laser

  • Thin and medium sheet in steel, stainless or aluminium.
  • Complex geometry, small holes and cut-outs.
  • Prototypes and short or medium runs: no part-specific tooling needed.
  • Parts that are bent afterwards: the cut is accurate and repeatable.

When another process is better

  • Plasma: structural work in thick plate where tolerance is not critical.
  • Waterjet: materials that cannot take heat (plastics, composites, stone) or thick metals where a heat-affected zone is not acceptable. Its minimum hole is around 1.8 mm.
  • Punching: medium and long runs with repetitive, standard shapes; it also allows small forms, embossing and threads on the same machine.

In short

If your part is sheet up to a few millimetres thick, has complex outlines or you need it in small quantities, laser is usually the most accurate and economical option. Our machines cut up to 6 mm. If in doubt, upload the file and we will tell you.
