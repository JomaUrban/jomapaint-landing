Sheet sizes: standard dimensions (1000×2000, 1250×2500, 1500×3000) and part size

The 1250x2500 and 3000x1500 sheet sizes are the ones you will find most often at a European stockholder. Knowing which sizes exist and what they weigh helps you design parts that nest well and know when a part is too big.

What are the standard sheet metal sizes?

Thin sheet is sold in three sizes shared by steel, stainless and aluminium.

Stock sheet sizes
Size (mm)AreaWeight per mm of thickness in steel

1250 × 25003.125 m²24.5 kg
1500 × 30004.5 m²35.3 kg

All three appear in Bucher Stahl's sheet and aluminium catalogues and in the cutting areas Xometry publishes for sheet up to 4 mm.

In thicker gauges there are more sizes. Xometry, for example, publishes 1500×3000, 2000×3000 and 1500×6000 mm for sheet over 4 mm. A size existing on the market does not mean every stockholder has it in every material and thickness.

How much does a 1250×2500 or 1500×3000 sheet weigh?

Weight = length × width × thickness × density. With 7.85 kg/dm³ for steel, 7.9 for 1.4301 stainless and 2.66 for 5754 aluminium:

Stock sheet doesn't measure exactly what it says

A 1250×2500 sheet leaves the mill with plus tolerances. For cold-rolled steel, EN 10131 allows at normal tolerance:

  • Width from 1200 to 1500 mm: 0 / +5 mm.
  • Length of 2000 mm or more: 0 / +0.3 % of the length, i.e. up to 7.5 mm on a 2500 sheet.
  • Flatness at thicknesses of 1.2 mm or more and widths from 1200 to 1500 mm: 8 mm.

On top of that, the actual thickness has its own tolerance; you will find it in sheet thicknesses and tolerances. If you need a rectangle with exact dimensions, don't buy the stock size: order sheet cut to size, cut all round.

Maximum part size

Our Bodor fibre laser cutting machines have a large-format bed. The practical limit for a part is the sheet it comes from: if your part is close to 1500×3000 mm, send us its dimensions when you order and we confirm whether we can cut it in one piece.

If the part doesn't fit, it is split. Design the joints from the start: an overlap with bolt holes, a tab and slot joint or a joint cover plate. The parts leave flat and unwelded; you or your supplier do the assembly.

Minimum part size: why it exists

Very small parts also cause problems, for reasons SendCutSend explains:

  • Getting them out of the sheet: very small parts are hard to separate without breaking or bending them.
  • Dross and burr: on small, precise cuts the laser goes more slowly, and in thick gauges that increases dross. Burr is also harder to remove on a small part.
  • Time: small parts take longer to cut per unit of area.

So they don't get lost, small parts are usually held in the sheet with micro-joints. If your part is very small, tell us and we tell you how we will cut it when we review the file.

Design with the sheet size in mind

  • Look at the limiting dimension. A part 1260 mm wide won't fit on a 1250 sheet and forces the use of a 1500×3000 one.
  • Think about how many fit. If a part takes up a little more than half a sheet, almost half is left over. Adjusting a few millimetres can fit two parts where only one fitted before.
  • Group parts in the same material and thickness in the same order: they are placed together on the sheet and the set-up is shared.
  • Don't draw the layout on the sheet yourself. Send one part per file with its quantity; we do the nesting.

Frequently asked questions

What are the standard sheet sizes?+

In thin sheet, 1000×2000, 1250×2500 and 1500×3000 mm. In thicker gauges there are also 2000×3000 and 1500×6000 mm sizes.

How much does a 2 mm 1500×3000 steel sheet weigh?+

About 70.7 kg, calculated with 7.85 kg/dm³. The real weight varies with the thickness tolerance.

What size is your bed?+

We cut on a large-format bed. If your part is close to 1500×3000 mm, give us its dimensions and we confirm whether it can be cut in one piece.

Will you sell me a whole uncut sheet?+

Cortelaser cuts parts. If what you need is a sheet to a specific size, we cut it to size all round.
