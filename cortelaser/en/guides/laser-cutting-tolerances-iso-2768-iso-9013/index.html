Tolerances on sheet metal parts

Asking for more accuracy than needed makes the part more expensive; asking for less may mean it doesn't fit. This is how to read the two standards used for cut sheet metal and what to put on the drawing.

Request a quote All guides

General tolerances: ISO 2768-1

When a drawing gives no tolerance on a dimension, the general tolerance stated in the title block applies. The most widely used is ISO 2768-1, with four classes: f (fine), m (medium), c (coarse) and v (very coarse). In sheet metal, class m is the usual reference.

Edge quality: ISO 9013

ISO 9013 classifies thermal cuts (laser, plasma and oxy-fuel) by two edge characteristics: the perpendicularity or angularity tolerance u, and the roughness Rz5. Each has ranges from 1 (the best) to 4 or 5, calculated from the thickness a in mm:

ISO 9013: perpendicularity and roughness ranges
RangePerpendicularity u (mm)Roughness Rz5 (µm)
10.05 + 0.003·a10 + 0.6·a
20.15 + 0.007·a40 + 0.8·a
30.4 + 0.01·a70 + 1.2·a
40.8 + 0.02·a110 + 1.8·a

Example with a = 10 mm: range 1 allows u = 0.08 mm and Rz5 = 16 µm; range 4, u = 1.0 mm and Rz5 = 128 µm. Formulas from technical guides summarising the standard; the full standard is paid for.

How to dimension the drawing

  • Put a general tolerance in the title block (for example, ISO 2768-m) and mark only the critical dimensions separately.
  • Dimension from a common datum, not in a chain: that way the tolerances don't add up.
  • If a face is visible or functional, mark it.

What accuracy costs

A tighter tolerance than needed has to be paid for: according to one fabricator, requiring ±0.05 mm makes cutting 20 % to 50 % more expensive. Ask for the accuracy the part needs and no more.

Frequently asked questions

What tolerance do you apply if I don't specify one?+

State it on the drawing or in the notes; if you don't, we confirm in the quote the tolerance we work to for your part.

Is laser more accurate than plasma?+

In thin sheet, yes. We compare them with figures in laser, plasma, waterjet or punching.
