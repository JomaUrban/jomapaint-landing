What we cut to size

Any flat part you can describe with dimensions. The most common requests:

  • Rectangular blanks and offcuts: a 450 × 300 mm sheet, a 2000 × 120 mm strip, a back panel for a cabinet.
  • Discs and rings: covers, flanges, large washers, round bases.
  • Plates with holes: anchor plates, base plates, drilled flat bars, access covers.
  • Rounded or chamfered corners, slots and slotted holes on any of the above.

The difference from buying a stock size is the dimension: a stock sheet has plus tolerances of several millimetres in width and length (we explain this in sheet sizes). Laser cut all round, the part comes out to the size on your drawing.

Materials and thicknesses up to 6 mm

  • Cold-rolled steel DC01, DC03 and DC04 (EN 10130).
  • Electrogalvanised steel DC01+ZE (EN 10152).
  • Galvanised steel DX51D+Z (EN 10346).
  • Stainless steel 1.4301 (304) and 1.4404 (316L).
  • Aluminium EN AW-5754 and 1050A.

We cut up to 6 mm. We confirm in the quote which thicknesses we have in each material. If your drawing uses another designation (St 12, AISI 304, AlMg3), check the equivalence table or just write it as it is.

What tolerance does cut-to-size sheet have?

Unless your drawing says otherwise, the general reference for untoleranced dimensions in sheet metal is ISO 2768-1 class m (medium). These are its values for the usual sizes of a cut-to-size sheet:

ISO 2768-1 class m: tolerance for linear dimensions
Dimension (mm)Class m tolerance
>30–120±0.3 mm
>120–400±0.5 mm
>400–1000±0.8 mm
>1000–2000±1.2 mm
>2000–4000±2 mm

Source: ISO 2768-1 table published by AMES. The thickness tolerance is that of the sheet, not of the cut.

If any dimension needs a tighter tolerance, state it on the drawing or in the notes and we confirm when reviewing the order whether we can meet it. There is more detail in laser cutting tolerances and, on thickness, in sheet thicknesses.

How to order without a DXF

For a rectangle or a disc you don't need to draw anything. In the quote form:

  1. 01Choose material, thickness and quantityAs with any order.
  2. 02Write the dimensions in the notesFor example: "Rectangle 450 × 300 mm, R5 corners, 4 holes Ø9 at 20 mm from the edges". Or "Disc Ø250 with Ø60 centre hole".
  3. 03If it is more complex, upload a PDF or a sketchWith all dimensions. We turn it into a cutting file and send you the drawing to approve before manufacturing.
  4. 04Get the priceIn writing, within 24 working hours.

If you already have the DXF, upload it: the form analyses it instantly. How to prepare it: preparing the file.

Price of sheet metal cut to size

The price depends on material and thickness, on the sheet area the part takes up, on cut length and on the number of holes, plus job set-up, which is spread across all the parts in the order. A simple rectangle has little cutting; the material weighs more in its price.

That is why ordering several identical parts, or several sizes in the same material and thickness in one order, lowers the price per part. We explain it in what determines laser cutting price.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order sheet cut to size without a drawing?+

Yes. Write the dimensions in the notes or upload a dimensioned sketch; if needed, we send you the drawing to approve.

What is the maximum thickness you cut to size?+

Up to 6 mm in all five materials. We confirm the available thickness in the quote.

What tolerance does cut-to-size sheet have?+

The general reference is ISO 2768-m; for example, ±0.5 mm on dimensions from 120 to 400 mm. If you need another, state it when you order.

Do you cut parts larger than a standard sheet?+

The part has to come out of one sheet. If it is close to 1500 × 3000 mm, send us its dimensions and we confirm whether we can cut it in one piece.

Do you bend the sheet?+

No. Cortelaser only cuts. If the part is going to be bent afterwards, send us the flat pattern.
