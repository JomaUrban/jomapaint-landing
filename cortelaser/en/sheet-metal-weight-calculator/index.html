How it is calculated

Weight is volume times density. With dimensions in metres and thickness in millimetres, it comes out directly in kilograms:

Weight (kg) = length (m) × width (m) × thickness (mm) × density (kg/dm³)

A part with holes or cut-outs weighs less than its rectangle: subtract the area of the openings or upload the DXF to the form, which calculates it from the real geometry.
