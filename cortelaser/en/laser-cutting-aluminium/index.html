Fibre laser cutting

Aluminium reflects a lot of infrared light and dissipates heat quickly. A fibre laser, with a wavelength of around 1 µm, is absorbed better than a CO₂ laser (10.6 µm), which is why it cuts aluminium well.

Even so, small holes need some margin: as a rule of thumb, a diameter of at least 1.5 times the thickness.

Finish

Parts leave cut, in mill finish and unpainted. If you are going to paint or anodise them afterwards, say so when you order so that we take the visible face into account.

Frequently asked questions

Do you anodise or paint?+

No. Aluminium parts are delivered cut, in mill finish.

Which alloy should I order?+

EN AW-5754 for strong parts, also if you are going to bend or weld them afterwards. If the part is decorative and lightly loaded, 1050A also works.

What about 6082 or 6061?+

They are extrusion and machining alloys more than sheet alloys. If your part needs one, say so when you order and we will consider it.
