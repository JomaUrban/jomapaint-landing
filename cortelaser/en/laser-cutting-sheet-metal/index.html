What we cut

Any flat part that can be drawn with closed outlines: flat bars, flanges, brackets, covers, perforated panels, signage, machine parts and flat patterns of parts that are bent afterwards. The laser cuts the outer outline, the holes and the internal cut-outs in a single pass, with no part-specific tooling.

There is no die to pay off, and changing part means changing program. That is why laser cutting fits anything from one unit to medium series.

  • One-off parts and prototypes.
  • Short runs and repeats of a part you have already ordered.
  • Flat patterns of parts that you or your supplier will bend, weld or paint.

The machines

We cut on Bodor fibre laser cutting machines, a Chinese manufacturer of this type of machine, with a large-format bed. The maximum thickness is 6 mm.

A fibre laser works at a wavelength of around 1 µm, compared with 10.6 µm for a CO₂ laser. The usual sheet metals absorb that light better and reflect less of it, which is why a fibre laser cuts aluminium well, while with CO₂ it is harder.

How fibre laser cutting works

A highly concentrated laser beam melts the metal at one point and a jet of pressurised gas blows the molten material out through the bottom. The head follows the part outline drawn by the program, which is calculated from your file.

The assist gas changes the result. With nitrogen, an inert gas at between 2 and 20 bar, the edge comes out oxide-free. With oxygen, the reaction adds energy and steel cuts faster, but it leaves an oxide layer on the edge.

If you are going to paint the part afterwards The oxide layer from oxygen cutting is not well bonded to the steel: paint grips on it, but it can flake off if damaged. If the part is going to be painted afterwards, say so when you order.

Quick design rules

These are general rules the industry uses so that the part comes out right first time. They are not limits of our machine: if your design falls outside them, we review it and tell you whether it can be made.

General design rules for laser cutting
FeatureRule of thumbWhy
Minimum hole in steel≥ 1 × thicknessBelow that, the hole comes out tapered or with dross.
Minimum hole in stainless and aluminium≥ 1.5 × thicknessThey are more reflective and conduct heat more.
Hole-to-edge distance≥ 1 × thicknessStops the edge from deforming or burning.
Distance between holes≥ 2 × thicknessLeaves enough material between cuts.
Minimum inside radius≈ kerf widthThe beam has a diameter: there are no perfectly sharp inside corners.

Source: design rules published by sheet metal fabricators (see sources). The laser cutting design guide has the details.

Edge quality and tolerances

The quality of a thermal cut is described by ISO 9013: edge perpendicularity and roughness, in four ranges depending on thickness. The part dimensions, unless the drawing says otherwise, usually refer to the general tolerances of ISO 2768.

If your part has critical dimensions, mark them on the drawing or in the notes with their tolerance. In the quote we confirm what we can guarantee. We explain it in the tolerances guide.

What makes a part cost more or less

Material and thickness set the base price. Then come cut length, the number of pierces (each hole is a start) and job set-up, which is spread across all the parts in the order.

That is why a one-off part costs much more per unit than the same part in a batch of twenty. The details, and how to make a design cheaper, are in what determines laser cutting price.

Frequently asked questions

What thicknesses do you cut?+

Up to 6 mm. State the thickness you need in the request and we confirm in the quote whether we cut it in that material and with what edge quality.

Do you bend or paint the parts?+

No. Cortelaser only does laser cutting: parts leave flat and unpainted. If you are going to bend or paint them afterwards, send us the flat pattern and tell us what you will do with them.

Do you cut tube or sections?+

This website is designed for flat sheet. If you need tube or sections cut, say so in the notes and we will get back to you.

Can I supply the sheet myself?+

Say so in the notes when you request a quote and we will consider it.

Do the parts have burrs?+

Laser cutting leaves a clean edge, but there may be some dross on the underside or the micro-joints that hold the part in the sheet. If you need deburred edges, say so when you order and we tell you whether we can do it.

Can you draw the part for me?+

If you have a dimensioned PDF drawing or a sketch with measurements, upload it: we turn it into a cutting file and send you the drawing for approval.
