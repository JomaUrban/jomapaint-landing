Orders and files

How do I request a quote?+

Upload your files in the form, choose material, thickness and quantity for each part, enter your details and send. We reply within 24 working hours.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. We only ask for your name and email to send you the quote.

What files do you accept?+

DXF and DWG for flat parts, STEP if you work in 3D and a dimensioned PDF if you have no CAD. See how to prepare the file.

Does the form send my files when I upload them?+

No. The DXF is analysed in your browser to show you dimensions and warnings; files are only sent when you click "Send request".

Can I order a single part?+

Yes. Bear in mind that the first part carries the job set-up: the unit price drops a lot with quantity.

Can you draw the part for me?+

If you have a dimensioned drawing or a sketch with measurements, upload it: we turn it into a cutting file and send it to you for approval.

Machines, materials and thicknesses

What machines do you cut with?+

Bodor fibre laser cutting machines with a large-format bed.

What is the maximum thickness you cut?+

Up to 6 mm. State the thickness you need and we confirm in the quote whether we cut it in that material and with what edge quality.

What materials do you cut?+

Five: cold-rolled steel (DC01, DC03, DC04), electrogalvanised steel (DC01+ZE), galvanised steel (DX51D+Z), aluminium (5754 and 1050A) and stainless steel (304 and 316L).

Do you bend or paint the parts?+

No. Cortelaser only does laser cutting: parts leave flat and unpainted. If you are going to bend them, send the flat pattern.

What tolerances do you work to?+

It depends on the material, the thickness and the part. Mark the critical dimensions on the drawing and we confirm what we can guarantee. See tolerances.

Do you do welding, tapping or other operations?+

This website is for sheet metal laser cutting. If your part needs another operation, say so in the notes and we will get back to you.

Lead times, delivery and payment

When do I get the quote?+

Within 24 working hours of sending the request.

How long does manufacturing take?+

The lead time is stated in writing in the quote and depends on material and quantity.

Do you ship the parts?+

You can collect them in Legutio (Álava, Spain) or ask for shipping; in that case we include it in the quote.

How do I pay?+

The payment method is stated in the quote.
