Glossary

Sheet metal glossary

The terms that come up in a quote or in a conversation with the workshop, explained in one or two lines.

CDEFGHIKMNOPRS
CCold-rolled
Steel sheet rolled at room temperature to its final thickness: accurate thickness and smooth surface. Grades DC01, DC03 and DC04 (EN 10130).
Cut-out
Internal opening cut in a part, as in a screen or a perforated panel.
DDC01
General-purpose cold-rolled steel (EN 10130), with a good surface. See cold-rolled steel.
Dross
Remains of molten metal that can stay stuck to the underside of the cut.
DXF
2D drawing exchange format. It is the usual file for laser cutting. See preparing the file.
EElectrogalvanised
Cold-rolled steel with a thin zinc layer deposited by electrolysis (EN 10152), for example DC01+ZE 25/25. See electrogalvanised.
FFibre laser
Type of solid-state laser used today for cutting sheet metal, with a wavelength of around 1 µm. Cuts steel, stainless and aluminium.
Flat pattern
Flat shape of a part that is going to be bent, before bending: it is what the laser cuts.
Fusion cutting
Laser cutting with an inert gas, usually nitrogen, which blows out the molten metal and leaves the edge oxide-free.
GGalvanised
Zinc-coated steel. Continuously galvanised sheet is designated, for example, DX51D+Z275. See galvanised.
HHeat-affected zone (HAZ)
Strip of material next to the cut whose properties change because of the heat.
IISO 2768
Standard of general tolerances for dimensions with no tolerance stated. Classes f, m, c and v. See tolerances.
ISO 9013
Standard that classifies the quality of thermal cuts by edge perpendicularity and roughness.
KKerf
Width of material the laser removes when cutting, usually 0.1 to 0.4 mm.
MMicro-joint (tab)
Small bridge left uncut so the part does not drop out of the sheet during cutting. Leaves a tiny mark on the edge.
NNesting
Arranging the parts on the sheet to make the most of it. Reduces material and cost.
OOxygen cutting
Laser cutting of steel with oxygen as the assist gas: faster, but leaves an oxide layer on the edge.
PPiercing
Initial hole the laser makes to start each outline. Every hole is a pierce and adds time.
RRz5 roughness
Measure of the roughness of the cut edge used by ISO 9013, in microns.
SSTEP
Standard exchange format for 3D models. Used to send the part if you work in 3D.
